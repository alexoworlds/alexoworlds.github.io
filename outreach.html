Outreach

Since October 2025, I am part of AstroBioLusitanos, a team of young portuguese scientists dedicated
to science outreach in the fields of astrobiology and space sciences.

The goal of our group is to communicate science in a simple, accessible and fun way, in order to inspire
portuguese students to follow carriers in the field of space sciences. To achieve that we manage
an Instagram account, under the handle @astrobiopt, and we organize workshops and public
talks in schools and universities around the country.
